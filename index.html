FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Straight answers, no fine print.
Is the demo really free?

Yes — 100% free with no obligation. We build a working demo of your future website, you look at it, and only decide after. If you don't like it, you owe nothing.

How fast can my website go live?

Most sites are live within 5–10 days of your approval. Simple sites can be faster — we'll give you an exact timeline with the demo.

Do I need my own hosting and domain?

We handle everything, including recommending reliable hosting with a discount through our partners. You can use your existing domain or we'll register one for you.

What if I already have a website?

Perfect — we audit it for free, show you exactly what's missing (mobile issues, no booking, no chat), and upgrade it without losing your content.

What exactly is and isn't included in a package?

Every package includes design, build, launch and unlimited revisions until you approve. Domain registration (~$10-15/yr) and hosting are billed directly to you at our partner discount — we add no markup and never charge hidden fees. Content writing for your pages is included; paid stock photography is not.

Are the portfolio sites real clients?

Our portfolio shows fully working DEMO PROJECTS we built to demonstrate our style. Real client projects are added here as they go live — we never show fake reviews or invented results.

Are free tools really enough? What are the limits?

We're upfront: free hosting and free tools have limits (storage, custom domains, advanced features). Where a free plan is enough, we use it and tell you its limits. Where your business needs more, we'll recommend a paid option — you pay the provider directly at the listed price, we never add markup. You'll always know what things cost and why.

What's included in maintenance?

Content updates, security, backups, small changes and priority support — from $49/month. Optional, cancel anytime.
